SOUTHERN CUSTOM PC CO. WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: complete responsive one-page website. Open it in a browser to preview.

Before publishing:
1. Open index.html in a text editor.
2. Find BUSINESS_EMAIL = "YOUR_EMAIL@example.com" and replace with the business email you want quote requests sent to.
3. Replace FACEBOOK_PAGE_URL with the exact URL of your Facebook business page.
4. Save and test the quote form. It uses the visitor's email app (no backend, database, or paid form service).

Free publishing option:
- Create a free account with a static website host such as GitHub Pages or Netlify.
- Upload/deploy index.html as the site. A custom domain is optional and usually costs extra.
- The website is static and mobile responsive.

Notes:
- Pricing shown is editable example starting labor/assessment and planning ranges, not verified local market quotes. Update before publishing if needed.
- The site does not take payments or store customer submissions.
